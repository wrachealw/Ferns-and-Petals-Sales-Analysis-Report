# Ferns-and-Petals-Sales-Analysis-Summary Report
## 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes Ferns and Petals sales data using SQL to identify sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and delivery efficiency. The analysis combines customer, order, and product data into a unified view for business insights.

## Business Questions
1. Total Revenue: Identify the overall revenue.
2. Average Order and Delivery Time: Evaluate the time taken for orders to be delivered.
3. Monthly Sales Performance: Examine how sales fluctuate across the months of 2023.
4. Top Products by Revenue: Determine which products are the top revenue generators.
5. Customer Spending Analysis: Understand how much customers are spending on
average.
6. Sales Performance by Top 5 Product: Track the sales performance of top 5 products.
7. Top 10 Cities by Number of Orders: Find out which cities are placing the highest
number of orders.
8. Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time: Analyze if higher order quantities impact delivery
times.
9. Revenue Comparison Between Occasions: Compare revenue generated across
different occasions.
10. Product Popularity by Occasion: Identify which products are most popular during
specific occasions.

## 3. Key Findings
**i. Overall sales:** The reported average order value is INR 3,520.99, with approximately 100 unique customers.

**ii. Monthly performance**: August was identified as a strong sales month, while January was reported as the weakest in the detailed monthly analysis.

**iii. Delivery performance:** Average delivery time was approximately 132 hours (5.5 days), with delivery durations ranging from 161 minutes to 15,630 minutes.

**iv. Popular ordering times:** 7 PM, 7 AM, and 9 AM recorded the highest order volumes.

**v. Top revenue-generating products:** Magnam Set, Quia Gift, Dolores Gift, Harum Pack, and Deserunt Box.

**vi. Best-selling product by quantity:** Dignissimos Pack, with 82 units sold.

**vii. Customer spending:** Female customers had a slightly higher average order value, while male customers purchased more units overall.

**viii. Top cities:** Imphal, Dhanbad, and Kavali recorded the highest order volumes.

**ix. Delivery time vs. order quantity:** The correlation coefficient of 0.0041 indicates virtually no linear relationship between order quantity and delivery duration. This means More quantity does not mean more time taken to deliver.

**x. Revenue by occasion:** Anniversary generated the highest reported revenue at INR 674,634 (19.2%), while Diwali generated the lowest at INR 313,783 (8.9%).

**xi. Product popularity by occasion:** Customer preferences varied across occasions, creating opportunities for targeted promotions.

## 4. Business Recommendations
1. Prioritize inventory for top-selling and high-revenue products.

2. Develop targeted marketing campaigns around major gifting occasions.

3. Use monthly sales trends to plan promotions and inventory.

4. Investigate delivery delays to improve fulfillment efficiency.

5. Target high-order-volume cities with localized marketing campaigns.

6. Personalize promotions based on customer preferences and purchasing history.


## 5. Conclusion
The analysis highlights opportunities for Ferns and Petals to increase revenue through better inventory management, seasonal marketing, and customer targeting. It also identifies delivery efficiency as an area for improvement.
